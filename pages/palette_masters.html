The long version

About this course

The Complete Painter's Path is a long, unhurried journey through oil painting — designed for people who want to fall in love with the medium rather than rush through it. There are no deadlines here, no grades, and no "correct" style to conform to. You paint when you can, as much as you want, and the course grows with you.

Over roughly 20 hours of video, you'll move through color, light, texture, composition and subject matter — but really, you'll be learning to see. We spend real time on the things most courses skip: how to mix a grey you actually like, how to leave a brushstroke alone, how to know when a painting is finished (and how to happily ruin one that isn't).

The difference

What makes it different

Most painting courses teach a method. This one teaches attention. Each section ends with a small, low-stakes invitation rather than an assignment — paint one object in three greys, or copy a passage from a painting you love, or simply sit with your materials for ten minutes. Nothing is graded. Everything is optional. Many students tell us the freedom is what kept them going when other courses felt like homework.

You'll also get occasional "studio letters" — short written notes on the practical life of a painter: setting up a workspace you'll actually use, buying materials without wasting money, finding time to paint alongside a full-time job, and dealing with the inner critic that tells you everyone else is better.

Before you begin

Good to know

Do I need special materials?
Start with a small kit: one brush set, three or four colors, and a board to paint on. A full recommended list is inside, but you can begin for under $60.
How long do I have access?
Forever, including every future lesson we add. No subscription, ever.
I'm a complete beginner — is that okay?
That's exactly who this is for. Nothing assumes prior experience, and there's no such thing as falling behind when there are no deadlines.
Is there feedback on my work?
There's a student gallery where you can share paintings if you'd like. The team highlights a few each month, but there's no obligation to post anything.
Why is this course cheaper than others?
We're a small collective with no academic overhead and no certificate program to run — we put everything into the lessons themselves. At $149 you get the same lifetime access and every future update for roughly half what comparable courses charge.
Do I get a certificate?
No. We focus purely on the painting, not the paperwork. If you need a formal certificate for a job or visa, a credentialed program may suit you better — but if you want to fall in love with oil painting without paying for admin, this is your course.
Can I pay in installments?
Yes — split the $149 into 3 interest-free payments of $49.67 at checkout. You get full lifetime access immediately after the first payment.
What if it isn't for me?
Email us within 30 days and we'll refund you, no questions asked.
