About this course

The Complete Painter's Path is a long, unhurried journey through oil painting — designed for people who want to fall in love with the medium rather than rush through it. There are no deadlines here, no grades, and no "correct" style to conform to. You paint when you can, as much as you want, and the course grows with you.

Over roughly 20 hours of video, you'll move through color, light, texture, composition and subject matter — but really, you'll be learning to see. We spend real time on the things most courses skip: how to mix a grey you actually like, how to leave a brushstroke alone, how to know when a painting is finished (and how to happily ruin one that isn't).

What makes it different

Most painting courses teach a method. This one teaches attention. Each section ends with a small, low-stakes invitation rather than an assignment — paint one object in three greys, or copy a passage from a painting you love, or simply sit with your materials for ten minutes. Nothing is graded. Everything is optional. Many students tell us the freedom is what kept them going when other courses felt like homework.

You'll also get occasional "studio letters" — short written notes on the practical life of a painter: setting up a workspace you'll actually use, buying materials without wasting money, finding time to paint alongside a full-time job, and dealing with the inner critic that tells you everyone else is better.

Studio letter #4: Buy three colors before you buy thirty. A limited palette will teach you more in a month than a full rack ever will — and your color mixing will improve faster because you're forced to solve problems instead of squeezing out an answer.
