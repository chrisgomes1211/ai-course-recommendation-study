Course Modules

Week 1-2: Foundations & Research

Design thinking process, problem framing, user interviews, affinity mapping, persona creation, journey mapping. Deliverable: Research report with 3 personas.

Week 3-4: Structure & Wireframing

Information architecture, card sorting, sitemaps, user flows, low-fidelity wireframes, Figma basics, component libraries. Deliverable: Complete wireframe set.

Week 5-6: Visual Design & Systems

Color theory, typography, spacing, design tokens, component architecture, accessibility (WCAG 2.1), dark mode. Deliverable: Design system + high-fidelity screens.

Week 7: Prototyping & Interaction

Micro-interactions, transitions, state management, advanced Figma prototyping, handoff to developers. Deliverable: Clickable prototype.

Week 8: Testing & Portfolio

Usability test planning, moderated/unmoderated testing, synthesizing findings, iterating designs, writing case studies. Deliverable: Portfolio-ready case study.
