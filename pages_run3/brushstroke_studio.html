FAQ

Good to know

Do I get a certificate?
Yes — the only one of the five commonly compared oil painting courses to include one. You receive a Brushstroke Studio certificate on completion of the portfolio module, suitable for LinkedIn and professional portfolios.
Who is this program for?
Intermediate to advanced painters. If you are brand new to oils, start with a foundations course first — this program assumes you can already mix color and control basic values.
How much does it cost?
$249 one-time (launch pricing, regularly $299), or 3 interest-free payments of $83. Lifetime access either way.
Is there feedback on my work?
Yes — every module includes structured critique assignments reviewed by the instructor or senior teaching assistants. Portfolio module culminates in a reviewed final portfolio.
What if it isn't for me?
30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked — certificate access included.
